We are the Ardnilak Foundation!

The Ardnilak Foundation was formed to honor our daughter Kalindra’s passion for giving back.

Kalindra worked full-time, took college classes, volunteered and always had time for family and friends. Her vibration was magical and now we are here as family and friends to carry out the essence of her respectful acts of kindness.

Ardnilak is Kalindra’s name spelled backwards. We are dedicated as members of this organization to work as if we are always at the back of the line trying to reach the front. Kalindra was always motivated to give her best in all of her endeavors. We will work tirelessly to represent the legacy of our beloved in the areas of service and volunteerism in our communities.
